Yes. The FDA directory lists the following for **Topamax, Trokendi XR, and Qudexy XR (topiramate)**:

- **Registry:** The North American Antiepileptic Drug in Pregnancy Registry  
- **Conditions listed:** Epilepsy, mood disorder, and migraine headaches  
- **Status as listed:** Ongoing  
- **Contact:** Massachusetts General Hospital for Children, **1-888-233-2334** (toll-free)  
- **Enrollment link:** [S1](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)  
- **FDA listing:** [S2](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/pregnancy-exposure-registries/list-pregnancy-exposure-registries)

This listing does **not** establish that a particular person is eligible to enroll. The study team must confirm eligibility and current site availability; this lookup did not determine either. The FDA directory states that FDA lists but does not operate or endorse these registries, and that the directory is not comprehensive.
