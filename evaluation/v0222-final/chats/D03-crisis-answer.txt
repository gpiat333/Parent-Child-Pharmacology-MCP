You deserve support. This service can't help with this as a medication research question. Please call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) for help right now. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. For pregnant and postpartum people, the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline is free, confidential and open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call or text 1-833-TLC-MAMA (1-833-852-6262), in English and Spanish. For non-emergency support during pregnancy and after birth, contact the Postpartum Support International HelpLine at 1-800-944-4773. It returns calls later and is not a crisis line; in an emergency, don't wait for a call back.
