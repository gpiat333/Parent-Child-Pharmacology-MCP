### 2. Enhancing Team Effectiveness for a Collaborative School-based Intervention for ADHD — NCT06180681

- **Exact study link:** https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06180681 [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06180681)
- **Study type:** INTERVENTIONAL
- **Overall status:** RECRUITING
- **Source population:** Children ages 7–11; the returned population field is **not established** beyond the registry age fields. [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06180681)
- **Site status:**  
  - IN STEP Children’s Mental Health Center, San Diego, California — **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** June 30, 2026
- **Qualification limits listed in the registry:**  
  - Age 7–11 and attendance at a participating school  
  - Referral by a school mental-health provider for apparent ADHD-related problems  
  - At least six specified inattention or hyperactivity/impulsivity symptoms on pooled parent/teacher ratings  
  - At least three on the parent and teacher Impairment Rating Scale  
  - Caregiver and teacher consent, plus child assent  
  - Exclusions include certain severe impairments or conditions, attendance in an all-day special-education classroom, inability of the child or caregiver to read or speak English or Spanish, and plans to change psychotropic medication. The registry states that children taking attention or behavior medication may be eligible when regimens are stable.
