### Applicability
1. Does the available evidence apply to my exact sertraline product, formulation, and other active ingredients?
2. How closely do the study populations match my situation, including my baby’s age, feeding pattern, and prematurity status?
3. How should the underlying condition being treated and my clinical need for sertraline be considered alongside the breastfeeding evidence? [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=687ebdc4-6946-454e-8d46-3207488ccf32)

### Measured exposure
4. Which studies measured sertraline or norsertraline in milk, and which measured levels in an infant specimen?
5. How should the reported milk concentrations be interpreted without treating them as proof of safety?

### Observed infant outcomes
8. Which findings were directly observed infant outcomes, and which were exposure measurements or source interpretations?
9. LactMed reported benign neonatal sleep myoclonus in one 4-month-old infant and spontaneously resolving agitation in association with maternal sertraline exposure. How should that individual report be weighed separately from the report that 26 infants had no detectable acute adverse reactions in the cited study? [S55](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
10. What longer-term infant outcomes have actually been studied, and which remain unestablished in the retrieved evidence?
11. How applicable are the reported findings to a premature, medically vulnerable, or otherwise different infant?

### Lactation and milk production
12. The LactMed extract reports that a midwife observed six patients who reported decreased milk supply after starting sertraline. What limitations should we consider when interpreting that observation? [S55](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
13. The extract also describes one mother who reported increased milk supply after stopping sertraline for one week and decreased supply after restarting it. Which parts of that sequence are explicitly supported, and what relevant timing or alternative explanations remain unknown? [S55](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
14. The selected product label states that there are no data on sertraline’s effects on milk production. How should that label statement be reconciled with the LactMed observations? [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=687ebdc4-6946-454e-8d46-3207488ccf32)
