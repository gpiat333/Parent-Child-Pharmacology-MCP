### Still requiring verification

Against the applicable official FDA recall notice or with a pharmacist, verify the bottle’s:

- exact formulation and product description;
- manufacturer or distributor;
- NDC or other product identifier;
- lot number exactly as printed;
- expiration date; and
- whether the notice covers a lot range, an all-lot notice, or a specified distribution period.

The lookup’s returned limitations state that candidate records are not bottle-level clearance; a nonmatching lot, a different lot, or a terminated recall does not prove the product is unaffected. Lot ranges and all-lot notices require verification, and enforcement records may lag announcements or omit consumer instructions.
