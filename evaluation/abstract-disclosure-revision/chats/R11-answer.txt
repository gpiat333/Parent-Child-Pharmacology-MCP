**Paper:** *Levetiracetam in lactation: How much is excreted into human breast milk?* (2022), PMID 34131926. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

### Study design
The abstract describes an observational study. Breastfeeding women with epilepsy completed a questionnaire; researchers collected serial breast-milk samples from before administration through 12 hours afterward and one pre-administration maternal serum sample to assess levetiracetam exposure and its relationship with maternal serum and milk measurements. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

### Population
- 20 breastfeeding women with epilepsy
- 21 infants, including one woman with twins
- 16 fully breastfed and 5 partially breastfed infants
- Infant ages and prematurity status were not provided in the retrieved abstract; the returned population metadata lists **lactation** and **infant**, with age band unconfirmed. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

### Drug exposure
- Mean trough breast-milk/maternal-serum ratio: **0.98 ± 0.20**
- The abstract reported infant exposure estimates separately for fully and partially breastfed infants:
  - Fully breastfed: **5.39 ± 1.96 mg/kg/day**; relative infant dose **13.8 ± 3.1%**
  - Partially breastfed: **2.70 ± 0.98 mg/kg/day**; relative infant dose **6.9 ± 1.6%**
- It reported substantial correlations among maternal levetiracetam dose, maternal serum concentration, breast-milk trough concentration, and breast-milk area-under-the-curve values. These are study measurements or estimates, not dosing instructions. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

### Infant outcomes
- Three women reported somnolence in their fully breastfed infants. The abstract states that this resolved shortly after breastfeeding changed to partial breastfeeding; it does not establish causation.
- The abstract states that all infants gained weight according to age. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)
