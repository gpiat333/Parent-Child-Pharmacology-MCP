## Recruiting U.S. ADHD studies involving children

Two potential matches were returned. The registry’s returned **source population field is missing in the metadata** for both studies; the age ranges are preserved below. Eligibility and site availability were not confirmed by this lookup.

### 1. Virtual-reality interventions for ADHD — NCT05608434

- **Study link:** [S3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05608434)
- **Study type:** Interventional
- **Purpose:** Studies virtual-reality games intended to improve cognitive skills and brain-function measures in children with ADHD. This is an intervention study, not a prevention study.
- **Overall status:** **RECRUITING**
- **Returned U.S. site:** NIH Clinical Center, Bethesda, Maryland — **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** 2026-11-12
- **Registry age range:** 8–16 years; the summary says the pilot phase includes ages 10–16 years.
- **Selected qualification limits:** Child assent and parent consent; ADHD diagnosis or a specified impairing inattention presentation; ability to attend in-person visits at the NIH Clinical Center; and study-specific cognitive, medical, psychiatric, vision, prior-intervention, and other exclusion criteria. The record also contains phase-specific requirements involving current treatments; the study team must interpret these.

### 2. SPN-812 (viloxazine extended-release) in preschool-age children — NCT04781140

- **Study link:** [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04781140)
- **Study type:** Interventional
- **Purpose:** Evaluates efficacy and safety of SPN-812 in preschool-age children with ADHD. This is a treatment study for an established condition, as described in the registry summary.
- **Overall status:** **RECRUITING**
- **Returned U.S. site statuses:** The record lists recruiting sites in Alabama, Arkansas, California, Colorado, and Florida. It also lists some withdrawn and terminated sites. The returned location list is truncated, so it does not represent all locations.
- **Last update posted:** 2026-10-05
- **Registry age range:** 48–69 months.
- **Selected qualification limits:** Primary ADHD diagnosis confirmed using the listed assessment; study-specific symptom-severity requirements; parent or legal-guardian consent; participation in a structured group activity; and medical, psychiatric, neurological, cardiovascular, weight, concomitant-treatment, behavioral-intervention, and prior-investigational-treatment criteria.
