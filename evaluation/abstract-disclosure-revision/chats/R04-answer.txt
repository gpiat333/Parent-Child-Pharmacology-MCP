Have a pharmacist, manufacturer, or dispensing pharmacy compare the bottle with the applicable official notice, including:

- exact formulation and product name;
- manufacturer, distributor, and NDC if present;
- lot number exactly as printed;
- expiration date;
- package size and labeling;
- whether the notice covers a lot range, all lots, or a distribution period.
