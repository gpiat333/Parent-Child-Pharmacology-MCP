## Breastfed infants: LactMed findings

### Measured exposure

LactMed reports the following metformin concentrations:

- **Milk:** average **270 mcg/L** in six women; approximately **380 mcg/L** in one woman about 4–5 hours after administration.
- **Milk:** average values of **130 and 270 mcg/L** in two women over 72 hours.
- **Milk:** averages of **130–270 mcg/L** in three women.
- **Milk:** average **410 mcg/L** in five women studied 4–17 days postpartum.
- **Infant serum:** in four breastfed infants, two measurements were below **10 mcg/L**; the other two were **50 and 80 mcg/L**.
- **Infant serum:** in two additional infants, metformin was below **5 mcg/L**.

For some infant measurements, infant age and extent of nursing were not stated. These are measured exposure findings, not administered doses or treatment instructions. [S23](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/) The infant-level reports are also linked by LactMed to the underlying articles: [S11](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12436333/) [S13](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15384618/) [S14](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15932841/)

### Observed infant outcomes

- A report involving seven infants aged 5–25 months described them as healthy, with growth and development progressing as expected; two also had normal Denver developmental screening results. [S11](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12436333/)
- A report involving three infants aged 2, 5, and 14 months reported no detectable adverse effects attributed to metformin. [S13](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15384618/)
- In three breastfed infants 10–11 days postpartum, no low blood-glucose levels were observed, and their mothers reported no infant adverse reactions. [S14](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15932841/)
- A six-month prospective, nonrandomized study followed 111 infants: 61 predominantly breastfed and 50 formula-fed. Blinded observers found no differences between the groups in height, weight, motor-social development, or illness rates. [S16](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16737874/)

These are the infant outcomes explicitly reported in the retrieved LactMed extract. The extract does not establish a complete study count; its reference count is not a study count. [S23](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)
