The FDA directory lists **The North American Antiepileptic Drug in Pregnancy Registry** for **Topamax, Trokendi XR, and Qudexy XR (topiramate)**, with **epilepsy, mood disorder, and migraine headaches** listed as conditions. Its status is **“Ongoing.”** [S2](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/pregnancy-exposure-registries/list-pregnancy-exposure-registries)

**Registry contact/enrollment link:** [https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)  
**Telephone:** Massachusetts General Hospital for Children, toll-free **1-888-233-2334**. [S1](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)

This confirms that a registry is listed; it does **not** establish that any particular person is eligible to enroll. Eligibility and current enrollment must be confirmed directly with the registry. FDA’s listing also states that it does not operate or endorse the registries and that its list is not comprehensive. [S2](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/pregnancy-exposure-registries/list-pregnancy-exposure-registries)
