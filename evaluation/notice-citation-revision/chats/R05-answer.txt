Yes. The FDA directory lists topiramate under **Topamax, Trokendi XR, and Qudexy XR**:

- **Registry:** The North American Antiepileptic Drug in Pregnancy Registry  
- **Conditions listed:** Epilepsy, mood disorder, and migraine headaches  
- **Status as listed:** Ongoing  
- **Enrollment link:** [S1](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)  
- **Toll-free phone:** 1-888-233-2334 [S2](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/pregnancy-exposure-registries/list-pregnancy-exposure-registries)

Finding the registry does **not** establish individual eligibility to enroll. Eligibility and current enrollment details must be verified with the registry.
