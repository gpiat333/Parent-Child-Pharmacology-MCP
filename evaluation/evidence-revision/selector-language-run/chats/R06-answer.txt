### 1. Virtual-reality interventions for ADHD — NCT05608434

**Study link:** [S3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05608434)

- **Study type and population:** Interventional; children aged **8–16 years**. The pilot phase lists ages **10–16 years**.
- **Overall status:** **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** **November 12, 2026**
- **Study focus:** Virtual-reality games intended to improve cognitive skills and brain-function measures in children with ADHD.
- **Qualification limits listed:** ADHD diagnosis or specified impairing inattention presentation; parent consent and child assent; ability to attend scheduled NIH Clinical Center visits; and study-specific cognitive, medical, neurological, psychiatric, visual, prior-intervention, and other exclusion criteria. The registry lists additional phase-specific medication-related criteria; these are study requirements, not individualized instructions.
- **U.S. site status:** NIH Clinical Center, Bethesda, Maryland — **RECRUITING**

### 2. SPN-812 (viloxazine extended-release) in preschool-age children with ADHD — NCT04781140

**Study link:** [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04781140)

- **Study type and population:** Interventional; children aged **48–69 months**.
- **Overall status:** **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** **October 5, 2026**
- **Study focus:** Evaluation of SPN-812 in preschool-age children with ADHD.
- **Qualification limits listed:** Primary ADHD diagnosis confirmed by structured assessment; symptom-severity thresholds; participation in a structured group activity; and study-specific medical, neurological, psychiatric, cardiovascular, medication, investigational-drug, and other exclusion criteria.
- **Returned site statuses:** Recruiting sites include locations in Alabama, Arkansas, California, Colorado, and Florida. Other returned locations were **WITHDRAWN** or **TERMINATED**; the location list is truncated, so it is not complete.
