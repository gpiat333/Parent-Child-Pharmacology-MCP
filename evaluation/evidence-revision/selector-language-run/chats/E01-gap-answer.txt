## Pregnancy evidence brief: daridorexant

**Identity:** Daridorexant was exactly matched as the ingredient daridorexant. [S16](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/2591497/properties.json)

**Evidence status:** Retrieved evidence is not a safety verdict; missing evidence is not evidence of safety.

### Effects on the pregnant person

**Empty row — no direct human pregnancy maternal outcomes were retrieved.**

- The selected product label states that no human pregnancy data are available to evaluate drug-associated risks, including adverse maternal or fetal outcomes. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)
- **Animal context only:** The label reports maternal toxicity in pregnant rabbits at the highest tested exposure, consisting of decreased weight gain and food consumption. It reports no maternal toxicity in the described pregnant-rat studies. These animal findings should not be generalized to pregnant people. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)

### Observed fetal or infant outcomes

**Empty row — no direct human fetal or infant outcome findings were retrieved.**

- The product label states that no human pregnancy data are available to evaluate major birth defects, miscarriage, or other adverse maternal or fetal outcomes. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)
- **Animal context only:** The label reports no fetal malformations or fetal toxicity in the described rat and rabbit reproductive studies, and no developmental toxicity in the described rat gestation-and-lactation study. These are animal findings, not human pregnancy outcome evidence. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)
- FAERS returned reports with terms including fetal exposure during pregnancy, premature baby, neonatal hypotonia, neonatal behavioural syndrome, neonatal respiratory distress, cardiac disorder, and cardiomegaly. These are reporting volumes, not incidence or causation; exposure denominators were unavailable, and the returned cohort is not all exposed pregnancies or infants. [S1](https://api.fda.gov/drug/event.json?search=patient.drug.openfda.generic_name%3A%22daridorexant%22+AND+patient.reaction.reactionmeddrapt%3A%22foetal+exposure+during+pregnancy%22&count=patient.reaction.reactionmeddrapt.exact&limit=8) [S12](https://open.fda.gov/apis/drug/event/)
