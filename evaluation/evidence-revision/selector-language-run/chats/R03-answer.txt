## Metformin: adult label warnings versus breastfed-infant findings

**Identity:** Metformin was matched exactly to RxNorm concept 6809. The retrieved label was **“METFORMIN HCL TABLET [QPHARMA, INC.]”**—one product label, not all metformin formulations. [S10](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/6809/properties.json) [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)

### Adult taking metformin: what the label says

The DailyMed label warns about **lactic acidosis**, describing it as rare but serious and associating risk with factors including renal dysfunction, hypoperfusion or hypoxemia, age, hepatic disease, dehydration, sepsis, alcohol use, and some interacting medicines. The label also reports adult adverse reactions including gastrointestinal effects and other reactions such as headache, asthenia, and hypoglycemia. These findings concern the adult label population and should not be treated as infant outcomes. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)

The retrieved adult **Warnings** text is truncated, so later portions of that section are missing.

### Lactation: what the label says

The label reports metformin excretion into milk in lactating animals and says comparable studies had not been conducted in nursing mothers. It identifies potential hypoglycemia in nursing infants as a concern. This is a label precaution, not an observed report that the infants in the label evidence developed hypoglycemia. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)

### Breastfed infants: what LactMed reports

**Measured exposure:** LactMed reports low metformin levels in milk. Metformin was undetectable in some measured infant serum samples and detectable at low levels in others. Infant ages, extent of nursing, or timing information were missing in some reports. Milk or infant serum concentration is an exposure measurement, not a clinical outcome. [S12](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)

**Observed infant outcomes:**

- **Seven breastfed infants, ages 5–25 months:** described as healthy, with expected growth and development; two also had normal Denver developmental screening results. The study’s design was not specified in the retrieved narrative. [S12](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)
- **Three breastfed infants, ages 2, 5, and 14 months:** no detectable adverse effects attributed to metformin were reported. The study design was not specified in the retrieved narrative. [S12](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)
- **Three breastfed infants, ages 10–11 days postpartum:** none had low blood-glucose levels, and mothers reported no infant adverse reactions. The study design was not specified in the retrieved narrative. [S12](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)
- **111 infants of 92 mothers:** a 6-month, nonrandomized prospective study followed 61 predominantly breastfed infants and 50 formula-fed infants; no differences were found in height, weight, motor-social development, or illness rates at 3 or 6 months. Because this was a mixed breastfed/formula-fed population, the result should not be generalized to exclusively breastfed infants. [S12](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)

### Missing or limited sections

- The retrieved DailyMed record covers one product label; the exact formulation should be confirmed before applying it to another product. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)
- The adult **Warnings** section was truncated.
- Structured pregnancy subsections—**Risk Summary, Clinical Considerations, Data, and Pregnancy Exposure Registry**—were not found in the retrieved label record. This is a missing or unparsed section, not evidence that the information does not exist elsewhere. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)
- In several LactMed reports, infant age, extent of breastfeeding, study design, exposure duration, or timing was not supplied in the retrieved narrative.
- The retrieved evidence does not establish that the label’s potential concern occurred in the reported infants, and the infant reports do not eliminate the label warning.
