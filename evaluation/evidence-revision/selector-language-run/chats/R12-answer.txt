### What they cannot establish

These counts:

- **Do not establish that acetaminophen caused an event.**
- **Cannot establish how often side effects occur, incidence, or individual risk**, because exposure denominators were unavailable.
- **Are not rates or probabilities**; they represent spontaneous-reporting volume.
- **Do not represent all infants**, because this query excluded records whose ages were not coded in months from 0 through 12.

Therefore, 117 vomiting reports means that 117 queried reports contained that coded term—not that 117 exposed infants vomited or that acetaminophen caused those reports. These are limitations of this MCP lookup and the returned FAERS data.
