**Observed symptoms reported in breastfed infants**

- **Diarrhea:** 3 of 25 mothers in one telephone follow-up reported diarrhea; another study reported diarrhea in 2 of 40 exposed infants.
- **Rash:** 1 of those 40 infants developed a rash. Another comparison study reported rash and sleepiness among reactions, with an overall reaction rate of 8.3%—similar to the comparator group.
- **Hives:** One infant had recurrent hives after breastfeeding while the mother was receiving amoxicillin/clavulanate and gentamicin. The specific causative drug could not be determined.
- **No adverse effects:** A separate study of 25 mothers reported no infant adverse effects.

[S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)

**Monitoring described in the record**

A controlled study asked mothers to monitor for tongue coating, feeding difficulty, stool changes, diaper rash, skin rash, weight change, and jaundice. These were **monitoring items**, not necessarily observed symptoms. The study found no statistical differences between infants whose mothers took related antibiotics and control infants. Thus, this passage should not be read as evidence that those symptoms commonly occurred or as individualized monitoring instructions. [S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)

**Uncertainty**

LactMed says occasional rash, diarrhea, or thrush have been reported but have **not been adequately evaluated**. The reports were small, some exposure details were incomplete, and at least one case involved other medicines; therefore, the observations do not establish that amoxicillin caused each symptom. LactMed reports low levels in milk, but published infant blood-level information was not found as of the record’s revision date. [S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)
