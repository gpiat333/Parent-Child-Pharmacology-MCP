Yes. The FDA directory lists the following registry for **Topamax, Trokendi XR, and Qudexy XR (topiramate)**:

- **Registry:** The North American Antiepileptic Drug in Pregnancy Registry  
- **Conditions listed:** Epilepsy, mood disorder, and migraine headaches  
- **Status as listed:** Ongoing  
- **Contact:** Massachusetts General Hospital for Children; toll-free **1-888-233-2334**  
- **Enrollment link:** [S1](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)

Finding a registry listing does **not** establish that a particular person is eligible to enroll. This lookup did not determine eligibility or verify current enrollment. The directory also states that FDA lists these registries but does not operate or endorse them, and that its list is not comprehensive.
