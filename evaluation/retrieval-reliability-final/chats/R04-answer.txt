### What still needs verification

Compare the actual bottle with the applicable official recall notice or ask a pharmacist/manufacturer to verify:

- exact product name and formulation;
- manufacturer, distributor, and NDC or other identifier;
- lot number exactly as printed;
- expiration date; and
- whether an **all-lot notice, lot range, repackaging period, or distribution condition** applies.

The lookup’s `NOTREAL-77` nonmatch and its candidate-record assessments are limitations or interpretations generated by this MCP implementation, not a manufacturer’s clearance or an FDA determination that the bottle was unaffected.
