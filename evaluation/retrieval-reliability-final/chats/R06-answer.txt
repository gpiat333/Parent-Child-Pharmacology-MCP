### 1. Virtual-reality interventions for ADHD — NCT05608434

- **Exact study link:** [S3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05608434)
- **Overall status:** RECRUITING
- **Site status:** NIH Clinical Center, Bethesda, Maryland — RECRUITING
- **Last update posted:** 2026-11-12
- **Study population:** Registry age range 8–16 years; source population field not established.
- **Study type:** INTERVENTIONAL
- **Focus:** Virtual-reality games intended to evaluate cognitive skills and brain function in children with ADHD. The registry indicates this is an intervention for an established ADHD condition.
- **Contacts:**  
  - Saadia A. Choudhury — (301) 437-0489, saadia.choudhury@nih.gov  
  - Daniel S. Pine, M.D. — (301) 594-1318, daniel.pine@nih.gov  
  - NIH Office of Patient Recruitment — 800-411-1222, TTY 711, ccopr@nih.gov
- **Qualification limits listed in the registry:** Parent consent and child assent; age requirements; ADHD diagnosis or specified impairing inattention presentation; screening assessments; ability to attend visits at the NIH Clinical Center; and exclusions involving certain cognitive, neurological, visual, psychiatric, medical, prior cognitive-training, and other investigator-assessed conditions. The pilot and main trial have different study requirements.
