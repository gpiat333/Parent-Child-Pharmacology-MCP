The label extract was missing the standalone warnings, warnings-and-precautions, boxed-warning, and drug-interactions sections; missing sections are retrieval limitations, not evidence that the information is absent from the full labeling. **Animal context only:** The label reported no maternal or embryofetal toxicity in the cited rat studies and no fetal toxicity or malformation in the cited rabbit studies.
