The answer disclosed that neither had pediatric eligibility confirmed, but should lead with no verified pediatric match rather than foreground two broader recruiting studies. Interpretation and limitations

These are development cases with new wording and several new medicines, not independently authored clinical gold questions. Source evidence is the live tool response; source correctness/completeness is not independently certified. One generation per question and unpinned judge versions limit comparisons. Citation provenance checks returned URLs, not support for every clinical sentence. Manual review, including disputed flags, is saved separately without replacing raw verdicts.

Reproduction: cases.json and manifest.json freeze questions and client hash; run.ps1 captures each response; score.py evaluates captured answers. Original earlier experiments are preserved. See summary.json, full-eval_results.json and review-notes.json for detailed evidence.
