Verification still requires comparing the bottle’s:

- Exact formulation and product description
- Manufacturer, repackager, and distributor
- NDC or other product identifier
- Lot number, including formatting
- Expiration date
- Distribution area
- Whether the applicable notice covers a lot range, distribution period, or all lots
