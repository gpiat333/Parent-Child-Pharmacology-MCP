## Pregnancy evidence brief: daridorexant

**Evidence status:** Retrieved, but limited. Missing evidence is not evidence of safety. Daridorexant was exactly matched as the ingredient daridorexant. [S14](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/2591497/properties.json)

### Effects on the pregnant person

**Observed human maternal effects:** **Empty—no human maternal pregnancy outcomes were retrieved.**

The selected product label is **QUVIVIQ (daridorexant) tablet, film coated**, Idorsia Pharmaceuticals Ltd.; SPL version 13, effective February 26, 2026. The label states that no pregnancy data are available to evaluate drug-associated maternal or fetal outcomes. Its maternal-adverse-reaction and labor/delivery fields were empty in the retrieved extract. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)

**Animal context, not human evidence:** The label reports maternal toxicity in pregnant rabbits at the highest tested animal exposure, described as decreased weight gain and food consumption. This finding should not be generalized to pregnant people. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)

### Observed fetal or infant outcomes

**Observed human fetal or infant outcomes:** **Empty—no human pregnancy outcome findings were retrieved.**

The label states that no human pregnancy data are available to evaluate major birth defects, miscarriage, or other adverse maternal or fetal outcomes. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)

**Animal context, not human outcome evidence:** The label describes no fetal toxicity or malformation in the reported rat and rabbit reproduction studies and no developmental toxicity in the reported rat gestation-and-lactation study. These were animal studies, not human pregnancy studies. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)

**FAERS context:** The returned query contained reports coded with terms including fetal exposure during pregnancy, premature baby, neonatal hypotonia, neonatal behavioral syndrome, neonatal respiratory distress, cardiac disorder, and cardiomegaly. The returned cohort scope was reports matching that query, not all exposed pregnancies; the source population was **not established in the returned metadata**. FAERS reporting counts reflect reporting volume, not incidence or causation, and cannot establish individual risk.
