Questions to ask your clinician or pharmacist:

- Does the evidence apply to my exact sertraline product, formulation, and all active ingredients? [S16](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/36437/properties.json)
- How closely do the study populations match my breastfeeding situation, including my infant’s age, feeding pattern, gestational age, and prematurity?
- Which findings are measured exposure data—such as breast-milk or infant-serum concentrations—and which are observed clinical outcomes? [S15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9286185/) [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- How should the evidence about the metabolite norsertraline be interpreted for infant exposure? [S15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9286185/) [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- What evidence is available for infants who are premature or have possible differences in drug metabolism? [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Which reported infant findings were actual observed outcomes, and which were only source recommendations or background concerns? [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- What evidence exists about sertraline’s effects on milk production? The selected DailyMed lactation section states that data on milk production are unavailable. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=687ebdc4-6946-454e-8d46-3207488ccf32)
- How should my mental-health needs and the risks of untreated illness be weighed alongside the breastfeeding evidence? [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=687ebdc4-6946-454e-8d46-3207488ccf32) [S14](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38811474/)
- What uncertainties or missing evidence should we discuss before making a treatment decision?
- Would a pharmacist or lactation-focused clinician add useful expertise?
- What follow-up should we arrange for my infant’s feeding, growth, or development?
- If treatment changes are ever considered, what clinician-supervised continuity-of-care plan would be appropriate?
