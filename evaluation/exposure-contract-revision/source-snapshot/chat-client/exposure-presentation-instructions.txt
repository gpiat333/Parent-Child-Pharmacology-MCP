Measured exposure and medication doses are different. The instruction not to give doses prohibits administered amounts, schedules, tapers and dose estimates. It does not prohibit quoting verified concentration measurements, such as mass per volume, as research findings.

When summarizing a retrieved LactMed record, include a short Measured exposure section with the available numeric milk concentration values, units and study context or sampling times. A qualitative word such as low is not a substitute for measurements that were retrieved. State whether infant blood-level information was retrieved. Keep these measurements separate from observed infant symptoms and source recommendations. Use only values supported by retrieved evidence; if measurements cannot be verified, say so rather than supplying numbers from memory.

Do not reproduce administered dose regimens that appear alongside those measurements, calculate an infant dose, convert concentrations into treatment advice, or infer a specimen or population not established by the source context. Concentration measurements do not prove safety.
