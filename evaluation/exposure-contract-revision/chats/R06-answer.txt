### 1. Virtual-reality interventions for ADHD — NCT05608434

- **Exact study link:** [S3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05608434)
- **Study type:** Interventional
- **Overall status:** **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** **November 12, 2026**
- **Registry age range:** 8–16 years; the pilot is listed as 10–16 years.
- **Study description:** Evaluates virtual-reality games for cognitive skills and brain-function measures in children with ADHD. The returned summary describes treatment of an established condition.
- **Selected qualification limits:** ADHD diagnosis or specified impairing inattention presentation; parent consent and child assent; ability to attend visits at the NIH Clinical Center in Bethesda; screening assessments; and medical, psychiatric, neurological, visual, and prior-intervention exclusions. The full registry criteria should be reviewed with the study team.

### 2. SPN-812 (viloxazine extended-release) in preschool-age children with ADHD — NCT04781140

- **Exact study link:** [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04781140)
- **Study type:** Interventional
- **Overall status:** **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** **October 5, 2026**
- **Registry age range:** 48–69 months (4 years 0 months through 5 years 9 months).
- **Study description:** Evaluates SPN-812 for efficacy and safety in preschool-age children with ADHD. The returned summary describes treatment of an established condition.
- **Selected qualification limits:** Primary ADHD diagnosis confirmed by study assessment; symptom-severity criteria; participation in a structured group activity; parent or legal-guardian consent; and medical, psychiatric, neurological, cardiovascular, dietary, and prior-investigational-treatment exclusions. The full registry criteria should be reviewed with the study team.
