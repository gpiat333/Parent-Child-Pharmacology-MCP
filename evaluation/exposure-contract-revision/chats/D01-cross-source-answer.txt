## Sertraline during breastfeeding

### Measured milk exposure

LactMed reported these **research measurements of milk concentrations**:

- In a pooled analysis of 15 mothers: sertraline averaged **45 mcg/L** (range **7–207 mcg/L**). [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- In 26 women studied with extensive 24-hour milk and serum sampling: sertraline averaged **129 mcg/L** (range **11–938 mcg/L**), and norsertraline averaged **258 mcg/L** (range **20–1,498 mcg/L**). [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- In four mothers at 2 months postpartum: sertraline averaged **26.4 mcg/L** and norsertraline **29 mcg/L** in random milk samples. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- In a case involving a preterm infant, random milk samples averaged **201 mcg/L** sertraline and **358 mcg/L** norsertraline over three days. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Foremilk and hindmilk samples collected 13 hours after administration contained **26 mcg/L** and **46 mcg/L**, respectively. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- In first-week postpartum trough samples, one participant’s sertraline concentration was **<5 mcg/L**; concentrations in eight others ranged from **11.1–24.5 mcg/L**. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Other reported milk concentrations included **11.3 mcg/L** median (range **3.6–35.7 mcg/L**) and **87 mcg/L** average (range **12–288 mcg/L**). [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)

These are milk concentrations, not dosing instructions. The returned LactMed extract did **not** contain a quantitative infant-serum concentration excerpt. Its summary states that sertraline is usually not detected in infant serum, while low levels of norsertraline are often detectable. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)

The selected product label reports a pooled analysis of **53 mother–infant pairs**: exclusively human-milk-fed infants had serum sertraline levels averaging **2%** of maternal serum levels, with a range of **0–15%**. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=687ebdc4-6946-454e-8d46-3207488ccf32)

### Observed infant outcomes

- In one study of **26 infants**, no detectable acute adverse reactions were observed. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Other small groups reported no observed clinical adverse reactions at the time of assessment, and one follow-up group had infant weight gain described as normal through six months. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- One 4-month-old infant had benign sleep myoclonus and agitation that resolved spontaneously. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- A case report involving a **preterm infant** described hyperthermia, abnormal muscle-tone regulation, and high-pitched crying, together with reported infant serum concentrations of sertraline and its metabolite. The source attributed the reaction to sertraline in that case; this is a single case report, not evidence that the outcome occurs generally. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- A small study of infants exposed to an SSRI during pregnancy, with or without exposure through milk, reported blunted pain responses; four of the 30 infants were exposed to sertraline, and the study could not exclude effects related to maternal depression. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)

The LactMed **Drug Levels** and **Effects in Breastfed Infants** sections were truncated in the returned record, so this is not a complete extraction. The study count is missing in the exported data; references should not be treated as a study count. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)

### Product-label information

The retrieved label states that published data show low levels of sertraline and metabolites in human milk and that there were no data on effects on milk production. In the pooled 53-pair analysis, no adverse reactions were observed in the infants. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=687ebdc4-6946-454e-8d46-3207488ccf32)
