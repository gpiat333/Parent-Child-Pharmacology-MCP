### Lactation wording in the label

The label says animal studies found metformin in milk and that comparable studies had not been conducted in nursing mothers. It raises a potential concern about hypoglycemia in nursing infants. This is a **label warning/uncertainty statement**, not an observed infant outcome. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)

**Measured exposure — not an outcome:**

**Observed infant outcomes:**

- In a report of **7 infants aged 5–25 months**, the infants were judged healthy, with expected growth and development; two had normal developmental screening results.
- In a report of **3 infants aged 2, 5, and 14 months**, no detectable adverse effects attributed to metformin were reported.
- In a report of **3 breastfed infants 10–11 days postpartum**, no low blood-glucose levels or maternal reports of infant adverse reactions were reported.
- A prospective, nonrandomized study followed **111 infants**: 61 predominantly breastfed and 50 formula-fed. No differences were found at 3 or 6 months in height, weight, motor-social development, or rates of illness. This mixed feeding population should not be generalized to exclusively breastfed infants. [S12](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)
