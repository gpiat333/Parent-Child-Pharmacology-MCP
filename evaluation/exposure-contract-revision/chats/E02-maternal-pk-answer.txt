## 3. Observed fetal and infant outcomes

### Major congenital malformations

For **first-trimester lamotrigine monotherapy**:

- International Lamotrigine Pregnancy Registry: **2.2%** among **1,558 infants**; 95% CI 1.6%–3.1%.
- NAAED Pregnancy Registry: **2.0%** among **1,562 infants**.
- EURAP: **2.9%** among **2,514 exposures**; 95% CI 2.3%–3.7%.

The label states that these frequencies were similar to general-population estimates. These are registry populations, predominantly involving epilepsy, and should not be generalized to every indication, combination regimen, formulation, or pregnancy context. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

### Oral clefts

The NAAED registry reported isolated oral clefts in **3.2 per 1,000** among **2,200 infants** exposed early in pregnancy, described in the label as a threefold increase versus unexposed healthy controls. The label also states that this finding was not observed in other large international registries. A European case-control study reported an adjusted odds ratio of **1.45**, with a 95% CI of **0.8–2.63**. The retrieved evidence therefore shows a discordant signal rather than a consistent finding across datasets. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

A completed EUROCAT registry study record describes a case-control study of first-trimester lamotrigine monotherapy exposure and isolated oral clefts. Its registry summary reports no statistically significant increased risk compared with other defects, but the record has **no posted results**. Its returned population fields include **child, adult, and older adult**, with numeric age limits missing; this age metadata concerns registry screening, not the age of pregnant participants. [S4](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01055327)
