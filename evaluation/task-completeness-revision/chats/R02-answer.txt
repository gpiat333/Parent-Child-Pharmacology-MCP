### Why the evidence differs
- **Milk concentrations** are measured exposure data.
- **Infant observations** are clinical outcome reports.
- Detecting sumatriptan in milk does not establish that an infant experienced an effect.
- Conversely, no reported adverse effects in three infants does not prove that infant exposure was absent or that adverse effects cannot occur.
