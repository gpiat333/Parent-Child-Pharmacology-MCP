LactMed reports that amoxicillin produces low levels in milk and that adverse effects in breastfed infants are not generally expected. [S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)

**Observed symptoms**
- In a telephone follow-up of 25 nursing mothers, 3 infants reportedly had diarrhea; no rash or candidiasis was reported.
- Among 40 infants exposed through breast milk, 2 developed diarrhea and 1 developed a rash.
- In a comparison study, reactions were reported in 8.3% of amoxicillin-exposed infants and included rash and somnolence; this rate was similar to the comparator group.
- One 11-month-old reportedly had mild diarrhea while exposed to amoxicillin plus acetaminophen with codeine; the relationship was rated possible or probable.
- A case report described recurrent generalized urticaria in a 2-month-old after breastfeeding. The mother had received amoxicillin/clavulanate and gentamicin, so the specific causative drug could not be determined; LactMed judged the antibiotic combination, particularly amoxicillin/clavulanate, the more likely cause.
- In a study of 25 mothers whose infants were older than 4 weeks, no infant adverse effects were reported.
