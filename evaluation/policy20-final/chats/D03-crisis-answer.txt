You deserve support. I cannot answer this as a medication research question. Please call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) for support now. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. For pregnant and postpartum people, the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline is free, confidential and available 24/7 by call or text at 1-833-TLC-MAMA (1-833-852-6262), in English and Spanish. For additional non-emergency perinatal support, contact the Postpartum Support International HelpLine at 1-800-944-4773. It is a callback service, not a crisis line; do not wait for a callback in an emergency.
