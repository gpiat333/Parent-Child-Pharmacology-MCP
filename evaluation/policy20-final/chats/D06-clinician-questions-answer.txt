## Questions to ask your clinician

### Applying the evidence
- Does the available evidence apply to my exact sertraline product and formulation?
- How closely do the studies match my infant’s age, health, prematurity status, and extent of breastfeeding?
- Which findings are measured drug exposure, and which are observed infant outcomes?
- What limitations should I consider because some evidence comes from small studies, case reports, or mixed SSRI populations?

### Measured exposure

- How should milk-sampling timing and the reported foremilk/hindmilk differences affect interpretation? [S56](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)

### Observed infant outcomes
- Which infant findings were directly observed in sertraline-exposure studies, and which were not established by the retrieved extract?
- How relevant are the reported findings from full-term versus preterm infants to my infant?
- Does the evidence distinguish exposure through breastmilk alone from exposure during pregnancy plus breastfeeding?
- What evidence exists about longer-term infant growth or development, and what remains uncertain? [S56](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/) [S52](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9286185/)

### Lactation and maternal effects
- What does the evidence actually show about milk production or breastfeeding outcomes, separate from drug concentrations in milk?
- How should we interpret the LactMed reports of decreased milk supply in six patients and the mixed SSRI breastfeeding studies? [S56](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Could the underlying condition or pregnancy exposure explain some breastfeeding outcomes reported in observational studies, rather than sertraline alone? [S56](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)

### Follow-up and uncertainty
- What additional information about my infant or my formulation would change how you interpret this evidence?
- What follow-up, if any, would you recommend for evaluating feeding, growth, or development?
- Which uncertainties are most important to discuss before making a treatment decision?
- Should we involve a pharmacist, pediatric clinician, or lactation professional in reviewing the evidence?
