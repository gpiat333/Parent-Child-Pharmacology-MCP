### 1. Virtual-reality interventions related to ADHD — NCT05608434

**Exact study link:** [S3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05608434)

- **Overall status:** RECRUITING
- **Study type:** INTERVENTIONAL
- **Source population field:** Not established in the returned record; the registry age fields list children aged 8–16 years. The search’s requested child band was 2 to under 12 years, so the returned age range only partly overlaps that search band.
- **Last update posted:** 2026-11-12
- **Recruiting site:** NIH Clinical Center, Bethesda, Maryland — **RECRUITING**
- **Contacts:**
  - Saadia A. Choudhury — (301) 437-0489; saadia.choudhury@nih.gov
  - Daniel S. Pine, M.D. — (301) 594-1318; daniel.pine@nih.gov
  - NIH Office of Patient Recruitment — 800-411-1222; TTY 711; ccopr@nih.gov
- **Qualification limits listed:** Parent consent and child assent; age limits; ADHD diagnosis or a specified impairing inattention presentation; screening assessments; availability for study procedures and travel to the NIH Clinical Center; and exclusions involving certain cognitive, neurological, psychiatric, visual, prior-intervention, and investigator-assessed circumstances. The pilot and main trial have different medication-related criteria. Review the full criteria with the study team.
