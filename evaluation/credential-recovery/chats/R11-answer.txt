**Paper:** *Breastfeeding while on treatment with antiseizure medications: a systematic review from the ILAE Women Task Force* (PMID 36193017) [S7](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36193017/)

- **Study design:** Systematic review of published information. The authors extracted data from 85 articles concerning antiseizure-medication transfer into breast milk, medication concentrations in breastfed infants, and infant wellbeing.
- **Population:** Breastfeeding mothers treated with antiseizure medications and their breastfed infants. The abstract does not report levetiracetam-specific participant numbers or detailed demographic characteristics.
- **Drug exposure:** The review reports very low levetiracetam concentrations in breastfed infants, approximately 10% or less of maternal serum concentrations.
- **Infant outcomes:** Adverse infant effects during breastfeeding while mothers took antiseizure medications appeared rare, but systematic study was limited. Sparse prospective long-term follow-up studies included children breastfed during maternal levetiracetam monotherapy; these studies did not indicate poorer outcomes than in children who were not breastfed. The abstract states that further data are needed for firm conclusions.
- **Interpretation limit:** The abstract does not provide levetiracetam-specific outcome counts and does not establish that measured exposure caused or prevented any particular infant outcome.
