## Metformin: adult label warnings vs. breastfed-infant findings

### DailyMed label — adult taking metformin
**Selected product:** *METFORMIN HCL TABLET [QPHARMA, INC.]*; this is one product label, not all metformin formulations. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)

The retrieved adult warning section describes **lactic acidosis** as a rare but serious complication associated with metformin accumulation. It identifies increased risk in settings including kidney impairment, reduced tissue oxygenation or perfusion, dehydration, sepsis, certain heart-failure situations, liver disease, older age, and excessive alcohol intake. The label also reports gastrointestinal adverse reactions such as diarrhea, nausea/vomiting, flatulence, indigestion, and abdominal discomfort. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)

The label’s **lactation section** reports milk transfer in lactating animals and says comparable studies had not been conducted in nursing mothers. It raises potential concern about low blood glucose in nursing infants. This is a label warning or theoretical concern, not an observed infant outcome in that section. [S1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=06ade2e1-5981-4e3d-b7e9-7ff083eb4551)

### LactMed — breastfed infants
LactMed summarizes human lactation studies and reports:

- Low metformin levels in milk in the summarized studies.
- Metformin sometimes detectable at low levels in infant serum.
- No detected adverse effects in small groups of breastfed infants.
- No low blood-glucose measurements in three young infants in one study.
- In a prospective study of 111 infants—61 predominantly breastfed and 50 formula-fed—no differences were reported in growth, motor-social development, or rates of illness at 3 and 6 months. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)

These are measured-exposure and observed-outcome findings. They do not establish that every breastfed infant will have the same outcome, and the summarized studies have incomplete details for some infants, including unspecified breastfeeding extent or timing.

### Comparison

| Topic | DailyMed label | LactMed |
|---|---|---|
| Lactic acidosis | Warning concerning the adult taking metformin | No lactic-acidosis outcome is reported in the summarized breastfed-infant evidence |
| Gastrointestinal effects | Adult adverse reactions reported in the label population | These adult findings are not automatically infant findings |
| Infant low blood glucose | Potential concern stated in the lactation section | No low blood-glucose levels were observed in three young infants in one reported study |
| Infant exposure | Animal milk-transfer information; limited nursing-mother information in this label section | Human milk and infant-serum measurements are summarized |
| Infant clinical outcomes | Not an infant-outcome source | Small studies reported no detected adverse effects; one prospective study reported no differences in selected outcomes |
