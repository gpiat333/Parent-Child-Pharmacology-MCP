This can help identify patterns for further investigation, including reported symptoms and medication-use problems.

### What they cannot establish

- **How often side effects occur:** These are FAERS reporting counts, and the MCP explicitly cautions that they represent **reporting volume, not risk**. They do not provide the frequency or incidence of side effects among all exposed infants. [S1](https://api.fda.gov/drug/event.json?search=patient.drug.openfda.generic_name%3A%22acetaminophen%22+AND+patient.patientonsetageunit%3A802+AND+patient.patientonsetage%3A%5B0+TO+12%5D&count=patient.reaction.reactionmeddrapt.exact&limit=10)
- **Whether acetaminophen caused an event:** A report links acetaminophen exposure and a reported event in the database, but these data alone do not establish causation. - **Overall safety or absence of harm:** The queried results cannot establish that acetaminophen is generally safe, or that an event did not occur merely because it was not among these returned terms.
