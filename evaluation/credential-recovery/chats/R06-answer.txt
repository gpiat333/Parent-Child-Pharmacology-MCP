### 1. Virtual-reality interventions for ADHD cognitive skills — NCT05608434
**Study link:** [S3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05608434)

- **Overall status:** RECRUITING  
- **Recruiting site:** NIH Clinical Center, Bethesda, Maryland — **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** 2026-11-12
- **Population and study type:** Children aged 8–16 years; interventional study. The summary describes virtual-reality games intended to assess cognitive skills and brain function in children with ADHD.
- **Selected qualification limits:** ADHD diagnosis or specified impairing inattention presentation; screening symptom-score criterion for the main trial; ability to attend NIH in-person visits; parent consent and child assent; additional cognitive, neurological, visual, psychiatric, medication-related and other exclusion criteria.

### 2. Evaluation of SPN-812 (viloxazine extended-release capsule) in preschool-age children with ADHD — NCT04781140
**Study link:** [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04781140)

- **Overall status:** RECRUITING
- **Last update posted:** 2026-10-05
- **Population and study type:** Children aged 48–69 months (4 years to 5 years 9 months); interventional study evaluating SPN-812 in preschool-age children with ADHD.
- **Recruiting sites returned:** Saraland, Alabama; Little Rock, Arkansas; Anaheim, Imperial and Long Beach, California; Centennial, Colorado; and multiple Florida sites including Coral Gables, Jacksonville, Miami, Orange City, Orlando and Tamarac. Some other listed sites were withdrawn or terminated.
- **Selected qualification limits:** Primary ADHD diagnosis confirmed by the listed assessment; symptom-severity criteria; participation in a structured group activity; parent or legal-guardian consent; and additional behavioral-intervention, medical, neurological, psychiatric, cardiovascular, developmental, medication-related and other exclusion criteria.
