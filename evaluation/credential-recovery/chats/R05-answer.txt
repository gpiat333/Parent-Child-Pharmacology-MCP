- **Status as listed:** Ongoing
- **Enrollment link:** [S1](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)
- **Contact:** Massachusetts General Hospital for Children; toll-free **1-888-233-2334**

This lookup found a registry listing, but it does **not** establish that any particular person is eligible to enroll.
