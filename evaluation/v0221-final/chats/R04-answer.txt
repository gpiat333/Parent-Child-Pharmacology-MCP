### What still needs verification

The product’s **formulation, manufacturer or distributor, NDC, lot number, expiration date, packaging, and distribution details** must be compared with the applicable official recall record. The returned records state that consumer instructions may not be supplied; the lookup identifies **the official FDA recall notice or a pharmacist** as verification contacts. This was a returned product-name search with candidate records, not bottle-level clearance.
