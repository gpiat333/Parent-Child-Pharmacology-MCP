- **Exact study link:** https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06180681 [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06180681)
- **Study type:** Interventional
- **Overall status:** **RECRUITING**
- **Site status:**  
  - IN STEP Children’s Mental Health Center, San Diego, California — **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** June 30, 2026
- **Registry age range:** 7–11 years; standard age: CHILD; sex: ALL; healthy volunteers: false
- **Listed qualification limits:**  
  - Ages 7–11 years and attendance at a participating school  
  - Referral by a school mental-health provider for apparent ADHD-related problems  
  - Listed parent/teacher symptom and impairment thresholds  
  - Caregiver and teacher consent, plus child assent  
  - Exclusions include certain severe impairments or conditions, all-day special-education classroom placement, inability of the child or caregiver to read or speak English or Spanish, and plans to change psychotropic medication
