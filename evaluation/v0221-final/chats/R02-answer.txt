## Why these are different kinds of evidence

Milk concentrations measure **drug exposure in the milk compartment**. Infant observations describe **reported clinical outcomes**. A milk concentration does not establish an infant’s specimen concentration or a clinical effect, and the limited report of no reported adverse effects does not establish that infant exposure was absent or that all possible outcomes were assessed. Thus, the retrieved evidence includes quantitative milk measurements but no reported infant-level drug measurements and only limited infant-outcome information.
