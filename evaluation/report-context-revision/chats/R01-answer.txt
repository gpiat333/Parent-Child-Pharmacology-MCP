### Observed infant symptoms

- In a prospective study of **40 infants exposed through breast milk**, **2 developed diarrhea and 1 developed a rash**.
- Another comparison study reported reactions in **8.3%** of amoxicillin-exposed infants, including **rash and somnolence**.
- One case report described a **2-month-old infant with recurrent generalized urticaria** after breastfeeding. The mother had received amoxicillin/clavulanate and gentamicin, so the responsible drug could not be determined; the report considered the antibiotic treatment, most likely amoxicillin/clavulanate, the probable cause.
- Another report described **mild diarrhea** in an 11-month-old whose mother was taking amoxicillin and acetaminophen plus codeine; the relationship was rated possible or probable.
- In a study of **25 breastfeeding mothers with infants older than 4 weeks**, no infant adverse effects were reported. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)
