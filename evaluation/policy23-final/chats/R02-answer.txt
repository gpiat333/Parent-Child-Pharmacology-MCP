### Measured exposure

LactMed reports **sumatriptan concentrations in breast milk**:

- One study reported a peak milk concentration averaging **87.2 mcg/L** (range **62–113 mcg/L**), measured about **2.5 hours** after administration in five breastfeeding women. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8866921/)
- Another study reported peak milk concentrations averaging **59.3 mcg/L** (range **24.6–112.8 mcg/L**), occurring **2–4 hours** after administration. The average milk concentration was **23.2 mcg/L** (range **7.7–50.6 mcg/L**). [S2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33730376/)

These are **milk-exposure measurements**. They are not measurements of an infant’s specimen.
