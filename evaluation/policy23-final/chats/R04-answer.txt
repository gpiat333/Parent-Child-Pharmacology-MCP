### What still needs verification

Verify the bottle’s:

- exact product and formulation;
- manufacturer, distributor, or repackager;
- lot number, including possible printing or transcription differences;
- expiration date;
- NDC, UPC, package size, and labeling;
- correspondence with the complete official recall notice.

The lookup’s limitations state that candidate product-name matches are not bottle-level clearance; a no-match, different lot, or terminated recall does not prove the product safe; and lot ranges or all-lot notices require human interpretation. The records can also lag announcements and may not contain all consumer instructions.
