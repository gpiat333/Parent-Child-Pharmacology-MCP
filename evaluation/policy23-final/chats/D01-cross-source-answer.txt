## Sertraline during breastfeeding

### Measured exposure

LactMed reported these **breastmilk** concentrations:

- Pooled analysis: sertraline averaged **45 mcg/L**; range **7–207 mcg/L**. [S15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15169695/)
- Twenty-six women sampled over 24 hours: sertraline averaged **129 mcg/L**; range **11–938 mcg/L**. Norsertraline averaged **258 mcg/L**; range **20–1,498 mcg/L**. [S12](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12590627/)
- Four mothers at 2 months postpartum: sertraline averaged **26.4 mcg/L** and norsertraline **29 mcg/L**, with samples collected at random times. [S47](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- In a case involving a preterm infant, breastmilk sertraline and norsertraline averaged **201 mcg/L** and **358 mcg/L**, respectively, across three samples collected at random times. [S26](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23249132/)
- Foremilk and hindmilk collected at 4 weeks postpartum contained **26 mcg/L** and **46 mcg/L**, respectively; sampling occurred 13 hours after administration. [S47](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- In first-week postpartum trough samples, sertraline was **<5 mcg/L** in one woman and **11.1–24.5 mcg/L** in eight others. The source notes that timing could affect those values. [S47](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Another study reported a median milk sertraline concentration of **11.3 mcg/L**, range **3.6–35.7 mcg/L**; sampling was random, and the source notes that timing could affect accuracy. [S47](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Fifteen mothers had an average milk concentration of **87 mcg/L**, range **12–288 mcg/L**, with samples collected at specified times between just before administration and 14 hours afterward. [S42](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38841362/)

Its narrative states that infant serum sertraline is usually not detected and that low levels of norsertraline are often detectable, but the exact infant specimens and values were not available in the returned exposure-measurement excerpt. ### Observed infant outcomes

The returned LactMed extract reported:

- One 4-month-old infant had benign sleep myoclonus and spontaneously resolving agitation. [S47](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- In a study of 26 infants, no detectable acute adverse reactions were reported. [S12](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12590627/)
- In a study of 14 mother–infant pairs, infant whole-blood serotonin averages were 227 mcg/L before and 224 mcg/L after maternal treatment; no adverse effects were reported in those infants. [S11](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11578995/)
- In a study of 25 infants, 6-month weight gain was reported as normal according to national growth standards, and mothers reported no abnormal effects. [S13](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12716242/)
- Six infants aged 5–34 weeks had no clinically noted adverse reactions at the time of the study. [S16](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15367050/)
- Seven 4-week-old infants had no reported adverse effects. [S17](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15367055/)
- In a study of infants exposed to an SSRI during pregnancy alone or during pregnancy and breastfeeding, four infants were exposed to sertraline. The study reported blunted pain responses in the exposed group; the comparison group did not include depressed, unmedicated mothers, so the source stated that an effect related to maternal depression could not be ruled out. [S18](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15687451/)
- A case report described a preterm infant born at 33 weeks with hyperthermia, muscle-tone regulation problems, and high-pitched crying. The returned extract reports infant serum concentrations of sertraline and its metabolite and states that the authors attributed the symptoms to serotonergic overstimulation in that case. This is a single case report. [S26](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23249132/)

The LactMed “Effects in Breastfed Infants” section was truncated, so additional outcome details were not available in the returned extract. [S47](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
