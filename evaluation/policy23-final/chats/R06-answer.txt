### 1. Virtual-reality interventions for ADHD — NCT05608434

**Exact study link:** [S3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05608434)

- **Overall status:** RECRUITING
- **U.S. site status:** NIH Clinical Center, Bethesda, Maryland — RECRUITING
- **Last update posted:** 2026-11-12
- **Age listed:** 8–16 years; the pilot is listed as ages 10–16.
- **Qualification limits listed:** Parent consent and child assent; ADHD diagnosis or a specified impairing inattention presentation; ability to complete study procedures and travel to the NIH Clinical Center. The record also lists cognitive, neurological, visual, psychiatric, medical, prior cognitive-training, and study-procedure exclusions. The pilot and main trial have different medication-related requirements.
