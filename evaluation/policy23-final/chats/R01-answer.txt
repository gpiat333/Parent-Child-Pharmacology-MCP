**Observed infant symptoms**

- In a telephone follow-up of 25 nursing mothers, 3 reported diarrhea in their infants; no rash or candidiasis was reported in that study. [S18](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8498418/)
- In a prospective study of 40 infants exposed to amoxicillin in breast milk, 2 developed diarrhea and 1 developed a rash. The cited study concerns amoxicillin/clavulanic acid and cefuroxime during lactation. [S12](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16044108/)
- A study comparing amoxicillin-exposed infants with infants exposed to a macrolide reported adverse reactions in 8.3% of the amoxicillin-exposed infants, including rash and somnolence; the rate was similar in the comparison group. [S13](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19366316/)
- One case report described recurrent generalized urticaria in a 2-month-old after breastfeeding while the mother was receiving amoxicillin/clavulanic acid and gentamicin. The report could not determine which drug caused the reaction; LactMed judged the combination product the more likely cause. [S3](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1472-8206.2009.00689.x)
- In another report, an 11-month-old whose mother was taking amoxicillin with acetaminophen plus codeine experienced mild diarrhea. The attribution was rated possible or probable. [S15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40183105/)
- In a study of 25 mothers and infants older than 4 weeks, no infant adverse effects were reported. This is a reported study finding, not proof that adverse effects cannot occur. [S16](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41582095/)
