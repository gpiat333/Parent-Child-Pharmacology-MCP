These are research measurements, not dosing instructions. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- A PubMed abstract describing 12 breastfeeding women and 11 mother–infant pairs reported detectable sertraline in 3 infants and desmethylsertraline in 6; the abstract also reported no observed adverse effects in that study. The returned record’s source population includes **pregnancy, lactation, and neonate**; findings should not be generalized beyond that population. [S15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9286185/)
- LactMed also describes separate observations, including one infant with agitation that resolved spontaneously and a separate preterm-infant case involving hyperthermia, tone abnormalities, and high-pitched crying. The retrieved text attributes the latter case to sertraline, but this is a case-level causal attribution, not proof applicable to all infants. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
