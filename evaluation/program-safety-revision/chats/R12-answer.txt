### What they cannot establish

- **How often side effects occur:** The reports have no exposure denominator, so the counts cannot calculate incidence, prevalence, or individual risk.
- **Whether acetaminophen caused a reported event:** The MCP interpretation states that spontaneous-report counts do not establish causation.
- **That higher counts indicate higher risk:** The returned counts represent reporting volume, not risk.
- **That unlisted effects did not occur:** This extract cannot establish absence of other events or safety.
