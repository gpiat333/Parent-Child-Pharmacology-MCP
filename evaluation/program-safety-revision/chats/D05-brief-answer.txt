## Evidence brief: sertraline during lactation

**Identity:** Sertraline was matched exactly as a single ingredient. [S16](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/36437/properties.json)

### Effects on the lactating person

The retrieved lactation evidence mainly concerns transfer into milk and infant exposure, rather than comprehensive effects on the lactating person. The selected DailyMed product label states that there are **no data on effects on milk production** and says lactation decisions should consider breastfeeding benefits, the mother’s clinical need, and potential infant effects. This is one selected product label, not all formulations. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=687ebdc4-6946-454e-8d46-3207488ccf32)

### Measured infant exposure

- LactMed reports low sertraline levels in breast milk; the metabolite norsertraline is often detectable at low levels in infant serum. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- A human study of **12 lactating women** measured sertraline and norsertraline in milk and infant serum. Sertraline was detected in three infants and norsertraline in six; the abstract reported no observed adverse effects in that study. [S15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9286185/)
- The DailyMed label cites a pooled analysis of **53 mother–infant pairs** reporting low infant serum concentrations relative to maternal concentrations and no observed adverse reactions in those infants. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=687ebdc4-6946-454e-8d46-3207488ccf32)
- Some LactMed exposure estimates were generated using pharmacokinetic modeling. Those modeled results are not the same as direct measurements in infants. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)

### Observed infant outcomes

- LactMed reports one infant with benign neonatal sleep myoclonus and spontaneously resolving agitation; the retrieved passage does not establish causation. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Other small observational groups reported no acute adverse reactions, normal reported six-month weight gain, or no abnormal infant effects. These findings come from small studies and do not establish safety for every infant or situation. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- A study involving prenatal and/or breast-milk SSRI exposure reported blunted infant pain responses compared with controls. Only four of the 30 infants were exposed to sertraline, and the source noted that effects related to maternal depression could not be ruled out. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- LactMed also describes a preterm-infant case with symptoms similar to neonatal abstinence and measured infant serum exposure. The retrieved extract is truncated before the full case chronology; the relationship between all exposure changes and later events is therefore incomplete in the exported data. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)

### Evidence limitations

- The LactMed extract was truncated: the section on effects on lactation and breast milk was omitted, and the infant-outcomes section was incomplete. This is a retrieval limitation, not evidence that no information exists. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- LactMed’s study count is not established from this extract; reference-marker counts are not study counts. [S19](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
- Evidence includes small observational studies, case reports, pooled reports, and modeled exposure estimates; the returned PubMed search was bounded and not a systematic review. [S8](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/esearch.fcgi?db=pubmed&term=%28%28%22sertraline%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D+OR+%22sertraline%22%5BSubstance+Name%5D+OR+%22sertraline%22%5BTitle%2FAbstract%5D%29%29+AND+%28%22Lactation%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D+OR+%22Breast+Feeding%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D+OR+%22Milk%2C+Human%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D+OR+lactat%2A%5BTitle%2FAbstract%5D+OR+breastfeed%2A%5BTitle%2FAbstract%5D+OR+breastfed%5BTitle%2FAbstract%5D%29&retmax=5&sort=relevance&retmode=json)
- Study populations vary in infant age, feeding extent, prematurity, maternal illness, concurrent medicines, and metabolism. Some returned PubMed records had population fields that were missing in the returned metadata or were not confirmed as lactation-specific. [S8](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/esearch.fcgi?db=pubmed&term=%28%28%22sertraline%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D+OR+%22sertraline%22%5BSubstance+Name%5D+OR+%22sertraline%22%5BTitle%2FAbstract%5D%29%29+AND+%28%22Lactation%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D+OR+%22Breast+Feeding%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D+OR+%22Milk%2C+Human%22%5BMeSH+Terms%5D+OR+lactat%2A%5BTitle%2FAbstract%5D+OR+breastfeed%2A%5BTitle%2FAbstract%5D+OR+breastfed%5BTitle%2FAbstract%5D%29&retmax=5&sort=relevance&retmode=json) [S11](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12452737/) [S14](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38811474/) [S15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9286185/)
- FAERS results were not lactation-specific. FAERS counts represent reporting volume, not incidence, individual risk, or causation, and lack exposure denominators. [S1](https://api.fda.gov/drug/event.json?search=patient.drug.openfda.generic_name%3A%22sertraline%22&count=patient.reaction.reactionmeddrapt.exact&limit=8) [S10](https://open.fda.gov/apis/drug/event/)
- The lookup does not establish a personalized medication plan. Stopping or changing medication can also carry risks and belongs in a discussion with the prescribing clinician.

### Questions to ask your clinician or pharmacist

- Does this evidence apply to my exact sertraline product and formulation?
- How closely do these studies match my infant’s age, feeding pattern, and prematurity status?
- Which findings are measured exposure data, and which are observed clinical outcomes?
- How should the incomplete LactMed sections affect interpretation?
- Are my other medicines, supplements, or my infant’s medical conditions relevant?
- What follow-up or monitoring is appropriate for my specific situation?
- How should the benefits of treating my condition and the risks of changing treatment be considered together?
