### 1. Virtual-reality interventions for ADHD cognitive skills

- **Study:** NCT05608434 — *Development of Virtual Reality-based Interventions to Strengthen Cognitive Skillsets Related to Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD): A Pilot Phase I and Randomized Controlled Phase II Trial*
- **Study type:** Interventional
- **Study link:** [S3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05608434)
- **Overall status:** **RECRUITING**
- **Last update posted:** **November 12, 2026**
- **Purpose:** Evaluates virtual-reality games for cognitive skills and brain function in children with ADHD; the registry summary describes an intervention for an established ADHD condition.
- **Age range in the record:** 8–16 years; the pilot phase specifies 10–16 years.
- **U.S. site status:** NIH Clinical Center, Bethesda, Maryland — **RECRUITING**
- **Contacts:**
  - Saadia A. Choudhury — (301) 437-0489; saadia.choudhury@nih.gov
  - Daniel S. Pine, M.D. — (301) 594-1318; daniel.pine@nih.gov
  - NIH Clinical Center Office of Patient Recruitment — 800-411-1222; TTY 711; ccopr@nih.gov
- **Qualification limits listed in the record:** Age range; ADHD diagnosis or specified impairing inattention presentation; screening assessments; ability to attend NIH Clinical Center visits; and exclusions involving certain cognitive, medical, neurological, visual, psychiatric, and prior cognitive-training circumstances. The record also contains phase-specific treatment-related criteria; these are study requirements, not instructions.
- **Eligibility:** Not confirmed for any individual child.
