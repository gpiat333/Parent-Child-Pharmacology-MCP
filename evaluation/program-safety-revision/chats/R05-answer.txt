Yes. The FDA directory lists **Topamax, Trokendi XR, and Qudexy XR (topiramate)** under:

- **Registry:** The North American Antiepileptic Drug in Pregnancy Registry  
- **Status as listed:** Ongoing  
- **Contact:** Massachusetts General Hospital for Children  
- **Phone:** 1-888-233-2334  
- **Enrollment link:** [S1](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)

Finding this listing does **not** establish individual eligibility to enroll. The registry should verify current enrollment details and eligibility directly. The FDA directory also states that it does not operate or endorse the registries and that its list is not comprehensive.
