Still verify with the official recall notice, pharmacist, manufacturer, or dispenser:

- Exact product and formulation, including naproxen versus naproxen sodium
- Manufacturer, repackager, and distributor
- NDC or other product identifier
- Lot number exactly as printed, accounting for formatting differences
- Expiration date
- Whether the applicable notice covers a lot range, all lots, or a distribution period
- Whether the bottle’s label and contents match the stated product
