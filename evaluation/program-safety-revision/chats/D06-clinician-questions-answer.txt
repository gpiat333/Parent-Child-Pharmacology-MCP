Here are questions to discuss with your clinician or pharmacist about **sertraline during breastfeeding**:

### Applying the evidence
- Does the evidence apply to my exact sertraline product, formulation, and any other active ingredients?
- How closely do the study populations match my breastfeeding situation and my infant’s age, feeding pattern, gestational age, and prematurity status?
- Which findings are measured exposure levels, and which are estimates or pharmacokinetic models?

### Infant exposure and outcomes
- What do the studies show about sertraline and its metabolite, norsertraline, in breast milk and infant blood?
- How should the small studies and the isolated case report involving a preterm infant be interpreted?
- What infant findings were actually observed, rather than merely listed as symptoms to monitor?
- How much confidence should we place in reports of normal growth or no observed adverse effects, given the small sample sizes and incomplete follow-up?

### Breastfeeding and milk production
- What evidence is available about effects on milk production or breastfeeding success?
