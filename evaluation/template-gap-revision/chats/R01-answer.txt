### Measured exposure
- In milk from **6 women**, concentrations were **0.69 mg/L** (range **0.46–0.88 mg/L**) at 4 hours and **0.81 mg/L** (range **0.39–1.3 mg/L**) at 5 hours after administration.
- **Infant-level measurements were not found** in the published information available to LactMed at its revision date. These are milk-exposure measurements, not infant outcomes or proof of safety. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)

### Observed infant symptoms
- **Telephone follow-up:** among 25 nursing mothers, 3 reported infant diarrhea; no rash or candidiasis was reported in that group.
- **Prospective study:** among 40 infants exposed through breast milk, 2 developed diarrhea and 1 developed a rash.
- **Comparison study:** adverse reactions were reported in 8.3% of amoxicillin-exposed infants, including rash and sleepiness; the reported rate was similar to that in infants exposed to a macrolide antibiotic.
- **Case report:** a 2-month-old infant developed recurrent generalized hives after breastfeeding following maternal treatment with amoxicillin/clavulanate and gentamicin. The specific causative drug could not be determined; LactMed considered the antibiotics in milk the likely source and amoxicillin/clavulanate more likely.
- **Another report:** an 11-month-old infant had mild diarrhea while breastfeeding during maternal use of amoxicillin plus acetaminophen/codeine; the association was rated possible or probable.
- **Study of 25 mothers with infants older than 4 weeks:** no infant adverse effects were reported. [S11](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)
