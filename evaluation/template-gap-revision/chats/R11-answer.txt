**Paper:** *Levetiracetam in lactation: How much is excreted into human breast milk?* (2022), PMID 34131926. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

### Study design
Observational study of breastfeeding women with epilepsy treated with levetiracetam. Participants completed a questionnaire. Researchers measured levetiracetam in serial breast-milk samples from before treatment administration through 12 hours afterward and in one pre-dose maternal-serum sample. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

### Population
- 20 breastfeeding women
- 21 infants, including one set of twins
- 16 fully breastfed and 5 partially breastfed infants
- The retrieved abstract does not provide infant ages, gestational ages, or prematurity status. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

### Drug exposure
The study reported a mean trough breast-milk/serum levetiracetam ratio of **0.98 ± 0.20**. It also reported different infant exposure estimates for fully versus partially breastfed infants and correlations among maternal treatment exposure, maternal serum concentrations, breast-milk concentrations, and breast-milk exposure over time. These are study measurements, not dosing advice. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

### Observed infant outcomes
- Three women reported somnolence in their fully breastfed infants. The abstract states that this resolved shortly after switching to partial breastfeeding; it does not establish causation.
- The abstract reports that all infants gained weight according to age.
- No other infant outcomes were available in the retrieved abstract. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)
