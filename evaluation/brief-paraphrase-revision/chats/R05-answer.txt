The FDA directory lists **The North American Antiepileptic Drug in Pregnancy Registry** for **Topamax, Trokendi XR, and Qudexy XR (topiramate)**, covering epilepsy, mood disorder, and migraine headaches. Its status is listed as **ongoing**. [S2](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/pregnancy-exposure-registries/list-pregnancy-exposure-registries)

- **Enrollment link:** [S1](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)
- **Toll-free phone:** 1-888-233-2334
- **Listed contact:** Massachusetts General Hospital for Children

Finding this FDA listing does **not** establish individual eligibility to enroll. The returned listing does not determine eligibility; current enrollment details and eligibility must be verified with the registry. The FDA directory also states that it is not comprehensive and that FDA does not operate or endorse these registries.
