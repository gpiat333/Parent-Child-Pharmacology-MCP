## Pregnancy evidence brief: daridorexant

**Identity:** The retrieved record matched daridorexant as a single ingredient. [S14](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/2591497/properties.json)

### Effects on the pregnant person

- **Human pregnancy findings:** No observed human maternal pregnancy outcomes were retrieved. The product label states that no human pregnancy data were available to evaluate drug-associated maternal or fetal outcomes. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)
- **Empty row:** No human maternal pregnancy outcome was retrieved.
- **Animal context, not human evidence:** The label describes animal reproduction findings, including maternal toxicity in rabbits at the highest tested exposure. These findings cannot be generalized to pregnant people. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)

### Measured fetal or infant exposure

- **Fetal exposure:** No measured fetal exposure data were retrieved; no fetal, cord-blood, or other pregnancy exposure measurements were reported in the retrieved evidence.
- **Infant exposure:** No infant exposure data were retrieved. LactMed was not used because it is a lactation-only source. [S17](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501922/)
- **Empty row:** No measured fetal or infant exposure was retrieved.

### Observed fetal or infant outcomes

- **Human outcomes:** No verified human fetal or infant outcome study was retrieved. The label states that no human pregnancy data were available to evaluate major birth defects, miscarriage, or other adverse maternal or fetal outcomes. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)
- **Animal outcomes, separately:** The label reports no fetal toxicity or malformation in the described rat and rabbit studies and no developmental toxicity in the described rat gestation/lactation study. These are animal observations, not human fetal or infant outcomes. [S7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3a2d1503-b816-40c9-9fac-7e03c5a3bcef)
- **FAERS reports:** The retrieved query returned spontaneous reports including “foetal exposure during pregnancy,” “premature baby,” neonatal hypotonia, neonatal behavioural syndrome, and neonatal respiratory distress. These counts represent reporting volume, not incidence or causation; the returned cohort is not a complete exposed population, and the source population and ages were missing in the returned metadata. [S1](https://api.fda.gov/drug/event.json?search=patient.drug.openfda.generic_name%3A%22daridorexant%22+AND+patient.reaction.reactionmeddrapt%3A%22foetal+exposure+during+pregnancy%22&count=patient.reaction.reactionmeddrapt.exact&limit=8)[S10](https://open.fda.gov/apis/drug/event/)
- **Empty row:** No verified human fetal or infant outcome study was retrieved.
