### What they cannot establish

- **Frequency or incidence:** The reports have no exposure denominator, so the counts cannot estimate how often an event occurs, its probability, or individual risk.
- **Causation:** A report involving acetaminophen does not establish that acetaminophen caused the event.
- **Comparative risk:** Larger reporting counts do not necessarily indicate a higher rate or risk.
- **Safety from non-reporting:** An event not appearing in this result does not establish that it does not occur.

The MCP specifically characterizes FAERS counts as **reporting volume, not risk**, and states that spontaneous reports do not establish causation and lack exposure denominators.
