## Lamotrigine pregnancy evidence

**Evidence status:** retrieved but not clinically validated; this is a bounded evidence review, not a systematic review or a safety verdict. Lamotrigine identity was verified. [S26](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/28439/properties.json)

### Maternal effects and underlying disease

- The selected DailyMed record is one product label—**LAMOTRIGINE KIT (LAMOTRIGINE) KIT LAMOTRIGINE TABLET**, OWP Pharmaceuticals—with an effective date of October 12, 2026. The pregnancy section is truncated, and DailyMed notes that in-use labeling may differ from the latest FDA-approved labeling. This is not all lamotrigine formulations. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)
- The label states that most pregnancy exposure data come from **women with epilepsy**. It separately describes epilepsy, with or without antiseizure-medication exposure, as being associated with preeclampsia, preterm labor, bleeding, placental abruption, poor fetal growth, prematurity, fetal death, and maternal mortality. These are **underlying-disease and seizure-control risks described in label clinical considerations**, not observed lamotrigine-specific adverse effects. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)
- For bipolar disorder, a 2025 **narrative review** of women with **bipolar disorder during pregnancy and postpartum** includes lamotrigine among treatments discussed. Its population should not be generalized to epilepsy. [S25](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40593436/)
- No specific maternal adverse-reaction findings were returned in the label’s structured pregnancy topics; that field is **missing in the exported data**, not evidence that maternal adverse effects were absent. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

### Measured exposure and pharmacokinetics

- The label reports that pregnancy-related physiological changes may affect lamotrigine concentrations and therapeutic effect, with reports of decreased concentrations during pregnancy and restoration of pre-pregnancy concentrations after delivery. This is label context, not an individualized dosing plan. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)
- A 2023 **naturalistic observational study** included **60 mothers** with a mixed source population of **pregnancy, lactation, neonates, and children**. It measured psychotropic-drug concentrations in serum and breast milk and followed exposed children for 12 months. The abstract reports lamotrigine-related breast-milk concentration findings, but the returned record does not provide a lamotrigine-specific sample size or fetal-exposure measurement. Those population details are **missing in the returned metadata**. [S22](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36103361/)
- A currently listed **observational, recruiting** study, NCT05450978, concerns pregnant **adult women with epilepsy** and examines antiseizure-medication clearance and exposure to unborn and nursing infants. Recruitment status is not evidence of benefit, safety, or individual eligibility. [S9](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05450978)

### Fetal and infant outcomes

#### Major congenital malformations

The label reports registry findings for **first-trimester lamotrigine monotherapy**:

- International Lamotrigine Pregnancy Registry: 2.2% of 1,558 exposed infants.
- NAAED Pregnancy Registry: 2.0% of 1,562 exposed infants.
- EURAP: 2.9% of 2,514 exposures.

The label states that overall malformation frequencies were similar to general-population estimates and that meta-analyses did not identify a consistent overall increase or a specific malformation pattern. These are registry populations of predominantly women with epilepsy and monotherapy exposure; they should not automatically be generalized to polytherapy or other indications. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

#### Oral-cleft signal

- The NAAED registry reported isolated oral clefts in 3.2 per 1,000 among 2,200 infants exposed early in pregnancy, described in the label as approximately three times the rate in unexposed healthy controls.
- The label also states that this finding was not observed in other large international pregnancy registries. A European case-control study reported an adjusted odds ratio of 1.45, with a confidence interval that included no association.

The returned evidence therefore contains a **single-registry signal with inconsistent replication**, rather than a settled conclusion. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

#### Other outcomes

The label says meta-analyses evaluated fetal death, stillbirth, preterm birth, small-for-gestational-age birth, and neurodevelopmental delay. It reports no data suggesting increased risk with lamotrigine monotherapy, while noting that differing outcome definitions, ascertainment methods, and comparator groups limit interpretation. This is a limitation statement, not proof that these outcomes cannot occur. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

The 2023 mixed-population observational study reported no significant differences in birth outcomes between in-utero-exposed and nonexposed newborns and no significant differences in development during the first 12 months. Because the returned abstract covers multiple psychotropic medicines and does not provide lamotrigine-specific outcome counts, this cannot be treated as a definitive lamotrigine-only result. [S22](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36103361/)

Animal findings in the label include fetal growth and skeletal changes, neurobehavioral findings, offspring mortality in one rat study, and reduced fetal folate concentrations. These are **animal observations**, not direct estimates of human pregnancy risk. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)
