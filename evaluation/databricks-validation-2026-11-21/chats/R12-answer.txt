### What they cannot establish

- **How often side effects occur:** FAERS lacks an exposure denominator, so these counts cannot calculate incidence, prevalence, or individual risk.
- **Whether acetaminophen caused an event:** Reports do not establish causation. They show co-reporting of a drug and an event, not a confirmed causal relationship.
- **Comparative risk or safety:** Reporting volume is not the same as risk and may be affected by exposure volume and reporting practices.
