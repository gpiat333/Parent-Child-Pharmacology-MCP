- **Adult taking metformin:** The retrieved lactation section does not report a specific adverse effect observed in the lactating adult. It focuses on milk transfer and possible effects in the nursing infant.
- **Infant warning:** The label says animal studies found metformin in milk at levels comparable to plasma and that comparable studies had not been conducted in nursing mothers in that label. It warns of a **potential for hypoglycemia in nursing infants**. This is a potential-risk statement, not a report that hypoglycemia occurred.
- The label also contains a historical **“Pregnancy Category B”** statement. FDA pregnancy-letter categories are historical and are not current FDA classifications. [S3](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/labeling/questions-and-answers-pregnancy-and-lactation-labeling-rule)

### What LactMed reports

LactMed is a lactation-only source. Its population field identifies the source population as **lactation**; pregnancy evidence is not included. [S4](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501020/)

- **Measured exposure:** Human studies summarized by LactMed found low metformin levels in milk. Metformin was sometimes detectable at low levels in breastfed-infant serum.
- **Observed infant outcomes:**  
  - Seven infants aged 5–25 months were described as healthy, with expected growth and development; two had normal developmental screening results.  
  - Three infants aged 2, 5, and 14 months had no detectable adverse effects reported.  
  - In three breastfed infants 10–11 days postpartum, none had low blood glucose, and mothers reported no infant adverse reactions.  
  - A prospective study included 111 infants: 61 predominantly breastfed and 50 formula-fed. At 3 and 6 months, the report found no differences between groups in height, weight, motor-social development, or illness rates.

These are observations in the reported study populations, not proof that adverse effects cannot occur. LactMed’s extracted record does not establish an overall study count; its reference count is not a study count. Some infant ages, extent of nursing, and exposure-timing details were missing in the source record.
