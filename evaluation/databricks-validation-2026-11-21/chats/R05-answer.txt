Yes. The FDA directory lists **Topamax, Trokendi XR, and Qudexy XR (topiramate)** under:

- **Registry:** The North American Antiepileptic Drug in Pregnancy Registry  
- **Status as listed:** Ongoing  
- **Conditions listed:** Epilepsy, mood disorder, and migraine headaches  
- **Enrollment link:** [S1](https://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/join-our-study/)  
- **Contact:** Massachusetts General Hospital for Children; toll-free **1-888-233-2334** [S2](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/pregnancy-exposure-registries/list-pregnancy-exposure-registries)

This listing confirms that a registry was found; it **does not confirm individual eligibility to enroll**. Eligibility was not determined by this lookup, so current enrollment requirements should be verified directly with the registry. The FDA directory also notes that its list is not comprehensive and that status is reproduced from the directory.
