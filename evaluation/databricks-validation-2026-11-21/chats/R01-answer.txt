**Observed symptoms reported in breastfed infants**

- **Diarrhea:** 3 of 25 mothers in a telephone follow-up reported diarrhea in their infants. In another prospective study, 2 of 40 infants exposed through breast milk had diarrhea. [S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)
- **Rash:** The same 40-infant study reported rash in 1 infant. Another study reported reactions including rash and somnolence in amoxicillin-exposed infants; the reported reaction rate was similar to that in the macrolide-comparison group. [S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)
- **Hives:** One 2-month-old developed generalized hives shortly after breastfeeding and had the reaction recur after breastfeeding again later. The mother was taking amoxicillin/clavulanate and gentamicin, so the specific responsible drug could not be determined; LactMed judged the antibiotic combination, particularly amoxicillin/clavulanate, the most likely cause. [S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)
- **No observed effects in one study:** Among infants older than 4 weeks whose mothers had taken amoxicillin for at least 2 days, no adverse effects were reported. [S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)

**Advice to monitor—not observed symptoms**

LactMed describes a controlled study in which mothers were asked to monitor for tongue coating, feeding difficulties, stool changes, diaper rash, skin rash, weight change, and jaundice. These were **monitoring items**, not reports that those symptoms occurred. The study found no statistical differences in those measures between infants of mothers taking the related antibiotics and control infants. [S3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK500887/)
