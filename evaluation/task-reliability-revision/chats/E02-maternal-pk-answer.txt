### Fetal and infant outcomes

The selected label summarizes pregnancy-registry findings, primarily for **first-trimester lamotrigine monotherapy**:

- International Lamotrigine Pregnancy Registry: major congenital malformations in **2.2% of 1,558 exposed infants**.
- NAAED Pregnancy Registry: major congenital malformations in **2.0% of 1,562 exposed infants**.
- EURAP: major birth defects in **2.9% of 2,514 first-trimester exposures**.

The label reports these overall malformation frequencies as similar to general-population estimates, while noting that differing outcome definitions, ascertainment methods, and comparators limit interpretation. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

For isolated oral clefts, the NAAED registry reported **3.2 per 1,000 among 2,200 infants exposed early in pregnancy**, described in the label as three times the rate in unexposed healthy controls. The label also states that this finding was not observed in other large international registries; a European case-control study reported an adjusted odds ratio of **1.45**, with a confidence interval including no association. These findings are therefore not consistent across the cited sources. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

The label states that meta-analyses evaluated fetal death, stillbirth, preterm birth, small for gestational age, and neurodevelopmental delay. The retrieved extract says that outcome differences were not established for lamotrigine monotherapy, but that differing definitions, ascertainment methods, and comparator groups limit conclusions. This is a stated evidence limitation, not proof that those outcomes were absent. [S10](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=474fb321-575c-4b97-9dbe-080061652ee4)

A completed EUROCAT registry record describes a case-control study of first-trimester lamotrigine monotherapy and isolated oral clefts. Its registry summary states that the study found no statistically significant increased risk compared with other defects, but the record has **no posted results**; the summary is not a complete study report. The returned source population is **congenital-anomaly registry cases/controls; case-control registry study; study size not stated in the returned record**. [S4](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01055327)

A naturalistic study reported no significant differences in birth outcomes between in-utero-exposed and nonexposed newborns and no significant differences in development during the first 12 months. Because the study covered multiple psychotropic medicines and the retrieved abstract does not make these findings lamotrigine-specific, they should not be generalized to lamotrigine alone. The returned population labels are **pregnancy, lactation, neonate, and child; observational study; 60 mothers**. [S22](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36103361/)
