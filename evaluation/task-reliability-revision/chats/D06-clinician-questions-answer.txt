## Questions to ask your clinician

**Applicability**
1. Does the available evidence apply to my exact sertraline product, formulation, and any other active ingredients?
2. How closely do the study populations match my situation, including my infant’s age, health, gestational age, prematurity, and degree of breastfeeding?
3. Were the infants exposed to sertraline alone, or were other medicines involved?

**Measured exposure**
4. Which studies measured sertraline or norsertraline in breast milk, and which measured infant levels?
5. How should the reported milk concentrations and occasional detectable infant levels be interpreted?
6. Is infant drug-level testing clinically indicated in my circumstances?

**Observed outcomes**
7. Which findings were actual observed infant outcomes, and which were source recommendations, planned measurements, or isolated reports?
8. How should findings from preterm infants be distinguished from findings in full-term infants?
9. What outcomes were assessed in the studies, and which important outcomes were not established by the available evidence?

**Breastfeeding and maternal effects**
10. What evidence is specific to sertraline rather than to antidepressants or SSRIs as a group?
11. What is known specifically about milk production or lactation, separate from sertraline concentrations in milk?
12. Could the underlying condition, postpartum factors, or other medicines affect breastfeeding independently of sertraline?

**Uncertainty and shared decision-making**
13. What important evidence is missing or limited?
14. What benefits of treatment and risks of untreated illness should be considered alongside infant-exposure findings?
15. What follow-up plan would address both my health and my infant’s health?
16. If treatment changes were ever considered, what clinician-supervised plan would account for both treatment-related and untreated-illness risks?
