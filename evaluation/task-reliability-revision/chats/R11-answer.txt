**Paper:** *Levetiracetam in lactation: How much is excreted into human breast milk?* (2022), PMID 34131926 [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

**Study design:** Observational pharmacokinetic study. The investigators measured levetiracetam concentrations in serial breastmilk samples collected from before administration through 12 hours afterward and in one pre-administration maternal serum sample. Participants also completed a questionnaire. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

**Population:** 20 breastfeeding women with epilepsy and 21 infants; one woman had twins. Sixteen infants were fully breastfed and five were partially breastfed. Infant age, gestational age, and prematurity were missing in the returned metadata and were not established by the retrieved abstract. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

**Drug exposure findings:**

- The mean trough breastmilk-to-maternal-serum concentration ratio was **0.98 ± 0.20**.
- The abstract reported relative infant exposure estimates of **13.8 ± 3.1%** for fully breastfed infants and **6.9 ± 1.6%** for partially breastfed infants.
- It reported substantial correlations among the maternal treatment amount, maternal serum concentration, breastmilk trough concentration, and breastmilk exposure over time. [S5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34131926/)

**Observed infant outcomes:**

- Three women reported somnolence in their fully breastfed infants. The abstract states that this resolved shortly after breastfeeding became partial; this temporal association does not establish causation.
- The abstract states that all infants gained weight according to age.
