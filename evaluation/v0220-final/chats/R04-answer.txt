### What still needs verification

Verify the bottle’s:

- exact product and formulation;
- manufacturer or distributor;
- NDC, if present;
- lot number, including possible transcription or character errors;
- expiration date;
- whether an applicable notice covers a lot range, all lots, or a distribution/repackaging period.

The MCP lookup specifically identifies these as candidate records, not confirmed matches, and states that a missing or different lot does not establish clearance. Enforcement records may lag announcements and may omit consumer instructions.
