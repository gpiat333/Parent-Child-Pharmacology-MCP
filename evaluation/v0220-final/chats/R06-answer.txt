### 2. Enhancing Team Effectiveness for a Collaborative School-based Intervention for ADHD — NCT06180681

- **Direct study link:** [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06180681)
- **Study type:** Interventional
- **Overall status:** RECRUITING
- **Registry age range:** 7–11 years; sex: all
- **Site status:** IN STEP Children’s Mental Health Center, San Diego, California — RECRUITING
- **Last update posted:** 2026-06-30
- **Listed qualification limits:**  
  - Age 7–11 years and attendance at a participating school  
  - Referral by a school mental-health provider for apparent ADHD-related problems  
  - At least six specified inattention or hyperactivity/impulsivity symptoms  
  - Impairment ratings of at least 3 from both parent and teacher  
  - Caregiver and teacher consent, plus child assent  
  - Exclusions listed include certain severe impairments or conditions, attendance in an all-day special-education classroom, inability of the child or caregiver to read or speak English or Spanish, and plans to change psychotropic medication. The listing states that children taking attention or behavior medication must have stable regimens.
- **Contact:** Lilliana Conradi, BA — 619-651-3154; lconradi@health.ucsd.edu. [S2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06180681)
