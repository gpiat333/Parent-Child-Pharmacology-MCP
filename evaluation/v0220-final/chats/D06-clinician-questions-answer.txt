### Effects on lactation
- What evidence exists regarding milk supply or other effects on lactation?
- How much weight should be given to the report of six patients who reported decreased milk supply?
- What information would help distinguish a medication-related observation from other possible explanations?

The retrieved LactMed extract reported a midwife’s observation that six patients reported decreased milk supply after starting sertraline. It also described one person’s report of increased supply after stopping sertraline for one week and decreased supply after restarting; the extract does not establish causation. [S54](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501191/)
