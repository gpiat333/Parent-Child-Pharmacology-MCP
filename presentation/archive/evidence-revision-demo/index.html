Measured exposure

LactMed reports that sertraline and its metabolite norsertraline were measured in breast milk. Across the summarized studies, calculated infant exposure was generally low, although estimates varied between studies. Infant blood testing usually found sertraline undetectable or at low concentrations; norsertraline was detected more often. These are exposure measurements, not clinical outcomes. S10

Observed infant outcome

One report described benign neonatal sleep myoclonus and spontaneously resolving agitation. S10

Product and scope

It lists adverse reactions and warnings concerning the person taking the medicine, including hypersensitivity reactions, serotonin syndrome, increased bleeding risk, activation of mania or hypomania, seizures, hyponatremia, and sexual dysfunction.
