User question

For sumatriptan during breastfeeding, what was measured in milk and what was actually observed in infants? If those are different kinds of evidence, explain that. No personal medication advice.

Saved response

Actually observed in infants
- A manufacturer correspondence report described three women who used sumatriptan while breastfeeding; none reported adverse effects in their infants. The doses and routes were unspecified.

How to interpret the difference
- Milk concentrations and estimated infant doses measure potential exposure.
- Reports about whether infants had adverse effects describe observed outcomes.
- They are not interchangeable: the milk data do not establish universal clinical safety, and the report of no adverse effects in three infants is limited evidence. S2 — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501255/
